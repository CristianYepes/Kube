## R4 — The game, as the agent sees it (rules read from the code) [read]

| Rule | Value | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Player move | `MOVE_SPEED 0.02` cells **per frame** (FPS-dependent) | `movement/player.c`, `cub3d.h` |
| Player turn | `ROT_SPEED 0.03` rad **per frame** | same |
| Enemy speed | `(0.012 + (idx % 4) * 0.003) * dt * 60` cells/frame (boss 0.01), capped at 0.4 | `bonus/mod/enemy_ai.c` |
| Waves | 1: 3 grunts, hp 2 · 2: 4 runners, hp 1 · 3: 1 boss, hp 8, and fog on | `bonus/mod/waves.c` |
| Spawn | a `'0'` cell at least 2 cells away, with line of sight to the player unless none exists | `bonus/mod/utils.c` |
| Enemy AI | IDLE -> CHASE; follows a BFS flow field toward the player; ATTACK when distance^2 < 0.8; back to CHASE when > 2.25 | `enemy_ai.c`, `flow_field.c` |
| Flow field guard | skipped if `map_width >= 64` or `map_height >= 64` (enemies then head straight at the player) | `flow_field.c` |
| Enemy damage | 10 hp per 1.0 s while ATTACK, distance^2 <= 1.0 and line of sight | `player_state.c` |
| Player weapon | hitscan: in front (dot > 0), `perp < 0.5`, range 10 cells, line of sight; nearest target; -1 hp; cooldown 0.6 s | `combat.c` |
| Line of sight | 0.1-cell ray march against walls and closed doors | `utils.c` `has_los` |
| End | hp <= 0 -> `G_OVER`; wave 3 with no enemies -> `G_WIN`; `R` restarts | `player_state.c`, `restart.c` |
| Randomness | LCG seeded from `gettimeofday` at init and on restart | `init_game.c`, `restart.c` |
| Doors | `E` toggles the cell in front; closed doors block enemies and line of sight | `doors.c` |
| Pickups / ammo | none; ammo is unlimited | whole tree |
| Keys | W/A/S/D move, arrows turn, Up arrow fires, E door, M minimap, R restart | `input_bonus.c` |

[calc] At 60 fps a grunt moves 0.72-1.26 cells/s, the boss 0.6 cells/s, and the player
1.2 cells/s. At the measured 44 fps the player drops to 0.88 cells/s while the enemies keep
their speed. **Because player speed depends on FPS and enemy speed does not, runs are not
comparable across maps or machines** unless the agent build fixes this.

## R5 — Jev constraints that apply (from `CONTEXTO_JEV.md` and the field notes)

- It cannot count or do arithmetic, or compare numbers reliably (§8.2). Angles and
  distances go in as words ("left", "close"), computed in code.
- It reads literally (§8.1). The instructions must name the exact state paths.
- Answers to different questions are not mutually coherent (§8.8). Combining independent
  axes (move + look + fire) invites contradictions: prefer **one** tactical Choice.
- A Noul threshold does not transfer to a Choice (§8.8).
- `confidence` summarizes how concentrated the distribution is. It is not the probability of
  being right (§6).
- Trained mostly on English: instructions and state keys in English.
- Pin the version and log the `model` field of every response (§3; field notes).
- Limits: 64k tokens per request, 32k for state plus the longest question (these change
  without notice). Rate limits **as of 2026-10-03: 100K tokens/s and 80 requests/s**
  (`docs.typesafe.ai/models.md`, read that day); on 2026-09-22 they were 250K tokens/s and
  1,200 requests/min. The cached `llms-full.txt` of 2026-10-02 still said 40 requests/s, so
  the individual page is the fresher source. We send about 2 requests/s, far below any of them.
- **Added 2026-10-03, still `jev-1.13`** (the docs, OpenRouter's catalog and PyPI show no newer
  model or SDK; `typesafe/jev-1.13` is served as `typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917`):
  - **Choice option order** (item 8 on the docs' jaggedness page, which numbers its list
    differently from §8 of `CONTEXTO_JEV.md`, where it is §8.10): `jev-1.13` leans toward the
    option listed first. The docs' advice is to reorder the options and check that the answer
    stays consistent. Our tactic Choice lists ENGAGE first; T8 measures the effect.
  - **The `confidence` formula is now published:** for a Choice with n options,
    `(p_max - 1/n) / (1 - 1/n)`. The docs also suggest `p_max` and the top-to-second ratio as
    simple alternatives computed from `probabilities`.

## R6 — Cost [calc]

- Token calibration from the 419 real `libros` requests (fork report): tokens ≈ 654 +
  characters / 3.77. That fixed part includes `libros`' long questions, so it is an upper-ish
  guide, not a measurement for this prompt.
- A state of about 1,500 characters plus one Choice of about 1,500 characters gives
  roughly 1,000-1,450 tokens per call, i.e. $0.000042-0.000061 per call.
- At 2 decisions/s: 7,200 calls/h, about $0.30-0.44/h. A 90-second episode costs about
  $0.008-0.011. Sixty evaluation episodes cost about $0.50-0.70.
- Only `usage.input_tokens` or `usage.cost` from real calls confirms any of this.

## Observed defects in the deliverable (scope of spec 001) [measured/read]

1. `maps/good/subject_map_sprites.cub` contains `'3'`, which no build accepts. Both binaries
   print `Error / Invalid character in map`, and `'3'` appears nowhere in `sources/`.
2. `maps/good/doors_test.cub`, `subject_map.cub` and `subject_map_sprites.cub` are bonus-only
   (`D`, `2`), so the mandatory binary rejects them. `maps/bad/forbidden.cub` is only bad for
   the mandatory binary: the bonus accepts its `'2'` as a sprite.
3. Player movement and rotation are per frame, while enemies use `dt` (R4). FPS was measured
   at 44-105.
4. `README.md:33-34` runs `maps/good/example.cub`, which does not exist.
5. Dead code or assets (checked with `grep` over `sources/`; the same search finds `put_pixel`
   in 10 files, so it can find things):
   - `render_weapon()` is an empty stub, still called every frame (`bonus/render/weapon.c`,
     `render/game_loop.c:48`).
   - `BREAK`, `CONTINUE` and `DEATH_TIME` are defined and used nowhere.
   - `E_DEAD` is tested in `enemy_ai.c:70` and `combat.c:55`, but nothing ever assigns it:
     dead enemies are removed from the array instead.
   - `t_sprite.anim_timer`, `.frame_idx` and `.type` are written in `set_sprite` and never
     read.
   - `textures/sprites/sprite_2.xpm` and `textures/shotgun/shotgun_fire3.xpm` (420x420) are
     loaded by nothing.
   - **Correction 2026-09-29:** `files/wall_cross.xpm` and `files/wall_pipes.xpm` were listed
     here as unused, and that was wrong. Only the good maps had been checked. **12 maps in
     `maps/bad/` and `forbidden.cub` use them.** They are test fixtures and stay.
     `tests/check.sh` found this (orphan check).
6. MiniLibX is cloned from `master` at build time, unpinned.
7. The game never calls `mlx_destroy_display`, which exists in the pinned MiniLibX
   (`mlx.h:118`), and never frees the `mlx` pointer. Hence the X11 connection's memory is
   still reachable at exit (R8). `mlx.supp` hides it.
   - On the path where the display opens and texture loading then fails
     (`maps/bad/tex_wrong_size.cub`), it is worse: **136 bytes definitely lost**, allocated in
     `mlx_init`, plus 77,805 still reachable (measured 2026-09-29).
8. The Makefile re-runs MiniLibX's `configure` and `make` whenever the `minilibx-linux/`
   directory looks newer than `libmlx.a`, because `$(MLX)` depends on the directory.
   - That depends on which write came last, so it shows up **intermittently**: 2 of 3
     samples with two makes each.
   - Bumping the directory's mtime reproduces it every time (3 of 3).
   - Nothing is rebuilt or relinked (R8).
9. `.gitignore` does not cover the build outputs (found 2026-09-29). After a build,
   `git add -A` would stage `cub3D`, `cub3D_bonus` and `minilibx-linux/`, the last one as an
   embedded git repository. Checked with a throwaway repo in the container.
10. GNU Make 4.3 checked with a minimal Makefile: when a library target's recipe always calls
   its sub-make (a `FORCE` prerequisite), make re-reads the library's mtime afterwards. The
   binary relinks only if the library really changed.
   `$(NAME)` does not list `$(LIBFT)` or `$(MLX)` as prerequisites, so a changed library
   would not relink the binary.
11. **Strafing was a third slower than walking** [measured and read 2026-10-05, fixed the same
   day]. `move_player` (`sources/movement/player.c`) moved forward and back along `dir`, which
   is a unit vector, but strafed along `plane`, which points the same way and is 0.66 long
   because it sets the field of view. So A and D ran at 1.2 × 0.66 = 0.792 cells/s [calc]:
   - measured through the agent bridge (spec 002, T3c): 1.20 forward and back, 0.79 sideways;
   - measured with `tests/check.sh`'s new probe: 2 s of A or D moved 1.60 and 1.59 cells in
     `cub3D` and 1.60 and 1.61 in `cub3D_bonus`, against 2.4 expected;
   - fixed on `feature/jev-agent` (`ad4244a`) by strafing along `(-dir_y, dir_x)`: the same
     probe gives 2.42 to 2.43 cells in both binaries, and `dev/dev.sh check` prints ALL
     PASSED.
